# Cooking and provisions — orchard, field kitchen and expedition meals

This batch adds five content JARs from the read-only ATM10 reference instance to Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249. Exact CurseForge project/file IDs, filenames, SHA-256, declared licenses and dependency ranges are in [`catalog/families/cooking-provisions.json`](../../catalog/families/cooking-provisions.json). Installed CurseForge metadata marks all five Client + Server and `allowModDistribution=true`. The JARs remain dependencies; no reference-pack quest, script, configuration, asset or narrative is imported. The family adds no new library: Titanium 4.0.50 and Patchouli 93, Sushi Go Crafting's required CurseForge projects, are already pinned.

These are different acquisition loops: fishing plus a preparation minigame, a field garden with Farmer's Delight cutting support, simple familiar-ingredient cooking, fruit orchards, and physical ingredient storage. [Herbs and Harvest's author](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mamas-herbs-and-harvest) describes 31 crops, 14 herbs and fruit trees. [Pam's Food Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pams-harvestcraft-2-food-core) adds about 180 foods based on vanilla ingredients. [Pam's Trees](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pams-harvestcraft-2-trees) is separately usable and offers 49 fruit-bearing trees. Storage Delight is a furnishing/storage addon, not a second digital-storage or cooking automation system. None is needed to receive, use or gift the original ration bundle.

## Additive ration routes

`tools/generate_cooking_provisions.py` emits `pack/kubejs/server_scripts/entrelumen_cooking_provisions.js` with two static `minecraft:crafting_shapeless` recipes:

| New recipe ID | Inputs (four occupied slots) | Native preparation evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `entrelumen:cooking_provisions/homestead_chicken` | 2 `farmersdelight:vegetable_soup` + 2 `pamhc2foodcore:chickendinneritem` | FD cooking pot makes soup from carrot, potato, beetroot and leafy green. Pam's chicken dinner consumes fried chicken, mashed potatoes and a vegetable. Fried chicken itself needs pot, flour, chicken and oil; mash needs baked potato, butter, salt and mixing bowl. |
| `entrelumen:cooking_provisions/field_supper` | 2 `farmersdelight:fish_stew` + 2 `herbsandharvest:beef_cheddar` | FD cooking pot makes fish stew from safe raw fish, tomato sauce and onion. The sandwich consumes **cooked** beef, two bread slices, cheddar, lettuce, mayonnaise and tomato. Herbs and Harvest's mayonnaise and bread-slice recipes were also checked. |

Both produce one `entrelumen:ration_bundle`. The existing `entrelumen:integration/travelling_pantry` remains exactly two vegetable soups plus two **cooked** Aquaculture fish fillets. The alternate routes therefore spend two prepared main dishes and two FD bowl meals for the same output, rather than shortcutting to raw ingredients. Shapeless vanilla crafting consumes the real FD soup/stew items, so their native bowl remainders must be preserved; no custom remainder override or item replacement is introduced. Static JSON cannot prove the returned bowls, so the full-pack recipe GameTest must craft each route, assert two bowls are returned, reject raw-food substitutions, and inspect AE2 crafting behavior separately.

The generator reads the selected JARs read only, checks their SHA-256, loader/dependency/license metadata and installed CurseForge identity, then checks eight exact native recipe sources and the unchanged original ENTRELUMEN route. In particular it inspects:

- `FarmersDelight-1.21.1-1.3.3.jar`: `data/farmersdelight/recipe/cooking/{vegetable_soup,fish_stew}.json`.
- `pamhc2foodcore-NEOFORGE-1.21.1-1.0.4.jar`: `data/pamhc2foodcore/recipe/{chickendinneritem,friedchickenitem,mashedpotatoesitem}.json`.
- `herbsandharvest-1.21.1-19.jar`: `data/herbsandharvest/recipe/{beef_cheddar,mayo,bread_slice}.json`.

Run `python tools/generate_cooking_provisions.py --check --source-instance 'G:/curseforge/Instances/All the Mods 10 - ATM10'` after integration, or omit `--source-instance` once `catalog/local-paths.json` maps all five exact pins. `--write` regenerates the owned KubeJS recipe script and nine worldgen overrides. The script checks collisions, output IDs and grid size, and logs a loaded-output receipt for all three ration recipes. A loaded-output receipt still does not establish ingredient matching, bowl return, recipe visibility, effect balance or survival pacing.

## Scope and acceptance risk

No new biome or dimension is selected. The three cultivation/fishing mods add features to existing biomes. The pinned JARs have 9 identical tree-species feature names in Herbs and Harvest and Pam's Trees: avocado, cherry, cinnamon, lemon, lime, orange, peach, pear and plum. Sushi Go Crafting also has an avocado tree feature. Pam's Trees has 50 configured tree features total; Herbs and Harvest has those nine plus a thistle feature.

The generator writes nine `{ "type": "neoforge:none" }` files at the **same Pam biome-modifier IDs** in `pack/kubejs/data/pamhc2trees/neoforge/biome_modifier/`. [NeoForge's 1.21.1 documentation](https://docs.neoforged.net/docs/1.21.1/worldgen/biomemodifier/) specifies that an overriding datapack file at the same path disables the original add-features modifier. The other 41 Pam placements, configured features, sapling recipes and loot tables remain untouched. Before suppressing each of the nine, the generator verifies its native `farmingforblockheads:market` recipe in the already selected Farming for Blockheads JAR. Its enabled `pamhc2trees:saplings` preset sells each sapling for one emerald. Pam's own recipe then makes a replacement sapling from eight matching fruits plus a sapling. This keeps the species obtainable and renewable without its natural insertion. Live Market availability and cultivated yield still need testing. Herbs' original placements remain; Sushi's avocado insertion remains because a separate non-worldgen Sushi sapling source is unproven.

Pam fruit also has a prepared-food use: `pamhc2trees:orangeitem` belongs to `c:fruits/orange`, included by `c:fruits`, which Pam Food Core uses twice in `fruitsaladitem` and three times in `fruitpunchitem`. The generator checks those native tag and recipe files. This is a real food bridge without installing Food Extended. Avoid adding another crop-garden stack: Pam's Crops would add 96 more crops, many overlapping Herbs and Harvest. Spawn density and generation cost of the remaining features still need live measurement.

The QA-only `CookingWorldgenGameTests` checks the **loaded** NeoForge biome-modifier registry: those nine IDs must resolve to `NoneBiomeModifier` and the other 41 Pam IDs to `AddFeaturesBiomeModifier`. It then looks up each native Market recipe through the live `RecipeManager` and uses Farming for Blockheads' `MarketPresetRegistry.isRecipeEnabled(MarketRecipe)` plus `getPaymentOrDefault()` to verify an enabled one-emerald sapling offer. It also crafts each native sapling from eight matching fruits and an ordinary sapling. These checks need a real full-pack server run; source JSON alone is not reported as runtime proof.

Sushi Go Crafting's author documents combined food effects and a discovery minigame. Those effects are optional food behavior, not required Ark power; live combat and food balance still need review before accepting the dependency. Storage Delight declares Minecraft `[1.21,1.21.1)` although its CurseForge file is tagged 1.21.1. The installed loader's compatibility matrix is expected to admit it as described in the catalog, but an actual startup is required. No performance or full-pack compatibility claim follows from metadata checks.

For a playable path, establish Farmer's Delight and the garden in Act I, cook either the original fishing ration or the homestead chicken route in Act II, use kitchen storage and optional sea cuisine at outposts in Acts III–IV, and keep orchard/food stock useful for settlement supply and habitation in Acts V–VI. All recipes stay usable regardless of act, origin or gift. Delivering a ration remains an explicit Atlas action; possession or crafting alone does not advance narrative authority. No new quests or compulsory buff loop are authored here.
