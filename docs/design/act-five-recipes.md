# Act V: the world we build

Three existing portable prototypes connect late industry, renewable production and habitation. After `atlas_voices`, `resilient_backbone` consumes one `ark_bus`, `renewal_engine` consumes one `renewal_engine`, and `settlement_supply` consumes one `habitation_contract`. The preserved `world_network` closure requires all three and consumes three paper plus one copper ingot. None grants another copy of its prototype. Explicit Atlas delivery advances the current team's record; gifts, crafting and ordinary item use remain unrestricted.

The original 24-quest EN/ES chapter follows Sera's crossed-out plan for a single gate supplying every home. Three independent supply routes make the settlement useful while preparing the Ark. Optional field exercises teach buffer recovery, bounded replenishment, readable stores and inhabited spaces. They are self-reported practice, separate from authoritative campaign deliveries. No machine, hive, crop plot or decorative block is dismantled to satisfy a project.

The Nature's Aura audit also inspected `AltarRecipe$Serializer` and `BlockEntityNatureAltar` bytecode in the pinned 41.9 JAR. The serializer exposes input, output, optional catalyst, aura and time; recipe selection matches input/catalyst. The altar collects supported Overworld or Nether aura without mapping those two metal recipes to one dimension. Its gold-to-tainted-gold recipe uses 3,000 aura and 300 ticks; iron-to-infused-iron uses 15,000 aura and 80 ticks. Absence of a JSON field alone was not treated as proof of a dimension rule. Offering-table construction and the spirit are separate requirements.

The Offering Table itself needs Fear and Sorrow tokens. Root additionally inspected `data/naturesaura/recipe/tree_ritual/{token_fear,token_sorrow}.json`: each oak tree ritual yields two. Fear uses a Nether aura bottle, gold leaf, rotten flesh, feather, bone and soul sand. Sorrow uses an Overworld aura bottle, gold leaf, a ghast tear, one accepted raw meat, glass and a fish. Neither token recipe consumes sky ingots or the Offering Table. Include ghast-tear gathering in expedition preparation.

No mandatory boss appears in these documented direct ingredient routes. That is a bounded source finding, not a claim about every machine setup or alternative recipe. AE2 has a conditional Mystical Agriculture calculation-press recipe as well; its crop setting was not validated, so the main tutorial uses the verified native meteorite/Inscriber route.

## Runtime boundary

The three static shapeless recipes occupy seven, eight and seven slots. In the actual 110-dependency server, each matched two independent grid orders, produced exactly one expected item and rejected a missing ingredient. The habitation contract returned exactly four `minecraft:bowl` remainders; the other recipes returned none. The input grids were unchanged by recipe assembly.

Twenty embedded-server GameTests and 29 unit tests passed, including exact consumption, offhand surplus, replay rejection, campaign isolation, saved-data restoration, FTB mirror authority and the V-to-VI transition. FTB loaded five chapters, 128 quests and 28 campaign task IDs. The 104 earlier quest definitions and translated text retain their frozen fingerprints. See [Act V runtime evidence](../verification/act-five-runtime.json).

The client received the matching files while stopped; it has not been launched for an Act V rendered review. Survival acquisition, complete installation exercises, the ending, scale targets and performance acceptance remain open. The server also still reports 85 pre-existing missing-item loot tables (80 More Machine, four Mekanism Extras and one ExtendedAE EMC interface); no new ones appeared relative to the Act IV run.
