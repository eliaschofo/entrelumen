# Builder utilities: measurement, material conversion and functional glass

This family adds five pinned NeoForge 1.21.1 content JARs to the existing construction stack. The exact filenames, SHA-256 values, CurseForge project/file IDs, license declarations, dependency ranges and sides are in [`catalog/families/builder-utilities.json`](../../catalog/families/builder-utilities.json). The read-only ATM10 `minecraftinstance.json` marks all five reference files distributable. Only these dependency JARs are selected; no reference-pack script, quest, config, art or layout is imported. All five mods are Client & Server. Create 6.0.10, Rechiseled 1.2.5, Chipped 4.0.2, SuperMartijn642 Core Lib 1.1.24 and Fusion 1.3.14a already have pins in the base catalog; Glassential declares Fusion client-side.

| Selection | Early role | Continuing role through the Ark | Actual bridge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Measurements 3.0.3](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/measurements/files/6956811) | Measure a shelter, farm plot or machine clearance with a tape rather than placing trial blocks | Repeat the same measurements for observatory axes, corridors and six Ark modules | One ordinary `measurements:tape_measure` recipe, using wool and iron; no block palette |
| [Rechiseled: Chipped 2.0](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rechiseled-chipped/files/7524185) | Reuse the already installed Rechiseled chisel to select Chipped variants | Convert salvaged building stock for remote repairs and Ark finishes | Adds Chipped blocks through native `chiseling_recipes` JSON; no new block palette or vanilla crafting recipe |
| [Rechiseled: Create 1.1.1](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rechiseled-create/files/8302843) | From Act II, drive the mechanical chisel in the Create workshop | Filter and convert building batches for settlements and Ark construction | `rechiseledcreate:mechanical_chisel` uses `create:andesite_casing` + `rechiseled:chisel`; kinetic processing converts members of a chiseling group, including Create materials |
| [Glassential Renewed 3.4.6](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/glassential-renewed/files/8851434) | From Act II, make visible redstone runs, luminous glazing and pass-through service access | Keep observation rooms lit and mechanisms legible without hiding circuits behind opaque walls | Native redstone, light and ghostly glass behaviors; no bespoke Atlas recipe or power gate |
| [Simply Light 1.5.3](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simply-light/files/6219029) | Add thin edge strips and switchable light to usable shelters | Flush service lighting in Ark machinery rooms and walkways | Native `simplylight:edge_light` and redstone-controlled illuminant blocks, distinct from Macaw's architectural fixtures |

The mechanical chisel is a real Create kinetic processor with an output filter, as described by [its author](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rechiseled-create). This warrants its extra Create-themed palette, although it does bring 242 blockstate JSONs, including windows. Those windows remain optional alongside the separate Macaw family. Rechiseled: Chipped instead adds a conversion bridge to blocks that are already selected; [the author's description](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rechiseled-chipped) confirms the chisel route. The pinned 2.0 filename embeds mod version `1.3` in `neoforge.mods.toml`; the SHA-256 and CurseForge file ID identify the exact artifact. Its groups are loaded from `data/rechiseled_chipped/chiseling_recipes/*.json`, outside vanilla crafting recipes. The `stone.json` group explicitly contains `chipped:carved_stone`. At runtime `owner()` is the Rechiseled datapack loader (`rechiseled:datapacks`), while `recipe()` identifies `rechiseled_chipped:stone`; the native test checks both plus membership and one-for-one conversion. A fabricated vanilla crafting ID would not test this integration.

## Native recipe and behavior checks

These IDs and ingredients were read from the pinned JAR recipe files. They are suitable for loaded `RecipeManager` checks after the family is installed; registration alone is not proof of crafting or block behavior.

| Mod | Native recipe IDs and result | Runtime behavior to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measurements | `measurements:tape_measure`: gray wool, yellow wool and `c:ingots/iron` produce one tape | Selecting two positions reports distance and clearing removes the measurement |
| Rechiseled: Create | `rechiseledcreate:mechanical_chisel`: shapeless andesite casing + Rechiseled chisel produce one | A powered mechanical chisel converts one known chiseling input to the selected output, with input consumed once |
| Rechiseled: Chipped | Native group `rechiseled_chipped:stone` in `chiseling_recipes`, including `chipped:carved_stone`; inspect the loaded group | The existing chisel converts a member of that group without duplicating input; check a real loaded group before choosing IDs |
| Glassential | `glassential:glass_redstone`: 8 glass + redstone block produce 8; `glassential:glass_light`: 8 glass + glowstone produce 8; `glassential:glass_ghostly`: 4 glass + ender pearl produce 4 | Redstone glass supplies level 15, light glass emits level 15, and ghostly glass has empty entity collision; test the loaded block states, not only the recipe files |
| Simply Light | `simplylight:edge_light`: 3 glowstone + 4 `c:stones` produce 6; `simplylight:illuminant_block_on`: stone/glowstone/redstone torch produce 4; `simplylight:illuminant_block_on_toggle`: one unlit block produces one lit block | Neighbor redstone changes the illuminant block's light state; verify both directions with loaded blocks |

Glassential's functional glass supplies signal, light and pass-through behavior. The final pin is official [3.4.6](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/glassential-renewed/files/8851434), downloaded directly from CurseForge's CDN. It replaces the reference-instance 3.4.5 before the first full-pack boot, keeping the same NeoForge/Minecraft/Fusion dependency ranges. Its changelog reports fixes to entity gravity glass, multiplayer paint synchronization and lit colorable panes; it also adds redstone-controlled Smart Glass. The selected native checks cover redstone, light and ghostly glass. Client rendering, paint synchronization and gravity still require direct verification.

Simply Light adds many colored shapes (160 blockstate JSONs and 234 recipe JSONs), but edge-mounted fixtures and switchable flush blocks serve paths and machinery rooms that decorative hanging lights do not. Nothing in a quest, module or construction recipe requires a color variant. Macaw's selected Windows/Fences/Lights family owns architectural openings and fixtures; this family does not duplicate its selection.

## Rejected alternatives and limits

- [Connected Glass 1.1.14](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/connected-glass/files/6811960) adds connected glass appearances (119 blockstate JSONs) without a new construction behavior. Rechiseled already offers connected materials and Glassential supplies useful glass behavior.
- [Cloud Glass](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloud-glass) offers 16 semi-opaque, no-collision colored blocks. Ghostly Glass already covers the pass-through use; the remaining distinction is visual.
- Chisel2 adds another chisel and 1,293 blockstate JSONs over Chipped plus Rechiseled. It does not solve a missing workflow here.
- Additional Lights overlaps both Simply Light and the incoming Macaw Lights. Its pinned candidate also declares `minecraft [1.21,1.21.1)` in the JAR, excluding the pack's 1.21.1 despite its reference-instance presence.

No recipe is rewritten and no construction material is required for expedition or Ark authority. This is a building utility lane across Acts I–VI, not a power progression lane. Source inspection established IDs, license/dependency metadata and the intended behaviors; only a loaded full-pack server and client can establish actual registration, kinetic conversion, visual rendering, redstone updates, collision and multiplayer behavior. The installed family now has bounded dedicated-server evidence in [the runtime receipt](../verification/arcane-building-runtime.json): eight globally selected recipes, glass signal/light/collision, lamp switching and the native Chipped conversion group passed. Actual kinetic chisel processing, tape-measure interaction, rendering and multiplayer painting remain unverified.
