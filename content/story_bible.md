# ENTRELUMEN narrative bible · v0.1

## Promise
EN: Rebuild a place worth returning to, then reconnect it to a world that remembers differently.
ES: Reconstruí un lugar al que valga la pena volver y reconectalo con un mundo que recuerda distinto.

The Atlas is an imperfect archive, not an omniscient quest dispatcher. Reconstruction produces permanent practical infrastructure. Mystery should change how a player interprets a project, never hide essential instructions. Player-facing instructions state what is consumed, what persists and how to retry. Spanish uses voseo; English uses direct, warm prose. No untranslated custom names embedded in recipes.

## Voices
- **Atlas / Atlas:** brief, sensory, incomplete observations. It can mistake a memory for a current place. Its uncertainty is explicit; mechanical instructions remain reliable.
- **Mara / Mara:** provisioner and community organiser. Practical generosity; asks who benefits from a machine. Cooking and habitation are infrastructure, not chores assigned to a lesser discipline.
- **Ivo / Ivo:** surveyor and instrument maker. Tests explanations against observations. Curious without dismissing magic; teaches measurement and controlled experiments.
- **Sera / Sera:** network maintainer. Speaks in consequences and repair options, avoids jargon until taught. Her records reveal why permanent dependency on one central system failed.

None is a living remote authority issuing endless errands. Their records disagree; the player reconstructs a picture through concrete projects. Avoid invented emergencies requiring offline maintenance.

## Six-act spine
1. **A Light Among Ruins / Una luz entre ruinas:** shelter and hospitality. The first signal answers from a missing tower. Build a kitchen, prepare a survey, assemble an instrument. Question: can memory point to a real place?
2. **The Lost Crafts / Los oficios perdidos:** contrasting methods solve the same problem. Mechanical precision, early power and introductory magic produce instruments the previous act still uses. Reveal: the old network depended on collaboration, not a superior universal machine.
3. **Routes of Exchange / Rutas de intercambio:** reliable transport and supplies. Resource farms unlock by capability; logistics retains mixed inputs. Reveal: the collapse began with interrupted local connections before the central failure.
4. **The Atlas's Voices / Las voces del Atlas:** expeditions recover contradictory records. Combat punctuates investigation and offers recoverable attempts. Reveal: the Atlas merged several restoration plans and cannot distinguish their authors.
5. **The World We Build / El mundo que construimos:** choose implementations for six functions and support them at scale. Reveal: rebuilding the old single point of failure would repeat its weakness. Redundant local workshops remain valuable.
6. **The Last Horizon / El último horizonte:** commission the Ark in persistent, recoverable phases. Combine engineering, magic, nature, exploration, logistics and habitation. Resolution: the Ark connects autonomous places; it does not consume the home base or demand perpetual upkeep. Postgame masters alternate solutions and expeditions.

## Editorial acceptance
Each chapter has a playable question and a lasting installation or capability. Every quest teaches a mechanism, asks a meaningful choice, checks a useful build or carries an earned story beat. Do not split one recipe into five near-identical possession checks. Optional mastery can deepen a mod without turning the main ending into mandatory completion of every installed mod. Diagrams and in-world verification follow prose authoring, not quest-count targets. Validate the first hour before expanding the 900-quest ambition.
